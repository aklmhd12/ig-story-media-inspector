# Privacy

- No analytics are included.
- No advertising trackers are included.
- No Instagram credentials are collected.
- No private Instagram cookies are uploaded.
- The tool stores only the last pasted Story URL in your browser's local storage for convenience.
- The bookmarklet's scan data stays inside the Instagram browser tab unless you explicitly copy a URL or export a JSON file.

To clear the saved Story URL, clear site data for your GitHub Pages site or edit/remove it from the input field and browser storage.
